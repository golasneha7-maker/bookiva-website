# 🌍 Booking Application & Platform

A comprehensive and user-friendly web application designed for seamlessly booking **Flights**, **Cars**, **Hotels**, and **Activities**. This platform serves as a one-stop destination for all travel and adventure needs.

---

## ✨ Features

* ✈️ **Flight Booking**: Search, compare, and reserve flights to destinations worldwide.
* 🏨 **Hotel Reservations**: Discover and book top-rated hotels, resorts, and stays with ease.
* 🚗 **Car Rentals**: Rent vehicles for daily, weekly, or road trip convenience.
* 🎟️ **Activities & Experiences**: Find and book local attractions, tours, and outdoor activities.
* 🗺️ **Destination Guides**: Explore detailed information and curated travel insights for top global destinations.
* 👤 **Owner & Developer Information**: Full transparency regarding the team/owner behind the platform.
